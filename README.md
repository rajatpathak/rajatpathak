## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **mobile-first engineer and architect** based in **Jaipur, India**, leading product engineering at **[Appentus Technologies](https://www.appentus.com)**.

I shipped my first Hello World app in **2009**, and for the last **8+ years** I've been building production mobile apps across Android, iOS, and cross-platform stacks — from architecture and delivery to mentoring teams that are just getting started.

- 📱 Primary focus: **Android (Java/Kotlin)**, **iOS (Swift)**, **Flutter**
- 🌐 Also ship with **React**, **React Native / Ionic**, **Node.js**, **PHP**, **Python**
- 🧠 Currently leveling up in **AI** and applying it to real product workflows
- 🤝 Open to **open-source collaboration**, mentoring, and knowledge sharing
- 💬 Ask me about mobile architecture, Flutter, native Android/iOS, or shipping apps at scale
